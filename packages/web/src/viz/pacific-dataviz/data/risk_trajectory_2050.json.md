# risk_trajectory_2050.json

The flood-risk spine: population exposed in low-elevation coastal zones (2020 → 2050) × IPCC AR6 relative sea-level rise at 2050 × flood-frequency amplification factor, per country.

- **Exposure layers:** [derived/population_by_country.json](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/derived/population_by_country.json) (SPC `DF_POP_PROJ`) and [derived/pop_lecz_by_country.json](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/derived/pop_lecz_by_country.json) (SPC `DF_POP_LECZ`).
- **Sea-level layer:** IPCC AR6 sea-level projections (FACTS full-sample workflow `wf_1e`, 20,000 Monte Carlo samples, SSP1-2.6 / 2-4.5 / 5-8.5, 17/50/83rd percentiles at 2050, relative SLR incl. vertical land motion) from the [public AR6 FACTS simulations on Google Cloud Storage](https://storage.googleapis.com/ar6-lsl-simulations-public-standard/). Pulled per tide gauge by [analysis/extract_slr.py](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/analysis/extract_slr.py); the 5 countries without a nearby gauge refined from the 1° gridded product by [analysis/refine_slr_gridded.py](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/analysis/refine_slr_gridded.py) → [derived/slr_2050_by_country.json](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/derived/slr_2050_by_country.json).
- **Amplification-factor method:** AF = 2^(SLR / D) with doubling interval D = 5–10 cm (tropical Pacific at the sensitive end) per [Vitousek et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-01362-7) and [Taherkhani et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17321-2). Illustrative regional factor, not site-calibrated.
- **Processing script:** [analysis/risk_trajectory.py](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/analysis/risk_trajectory.py) → [derived/risk_trajectory_2050.json](https://github.com/codyrushing/crushingviz/blob/main/packages/data/pacific-dataviz-challenge-2026/derived/risk_trajectory_2050.json).
